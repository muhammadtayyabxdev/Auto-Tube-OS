Getting Started with AutoTubeOS
📅 Updated May 14, 2026 ⏱ 5 min read 👁 12,400 views

Welcome to AutoTubeOS — the operating system for faceless YouTube creators. This guide will walk you through setting up your workspace in under 10 minutes.

Step 1: Create Your Account

1
Go to autotubeos.com/signup and enter your email
2
Verify your email address (check spam if needed)
3
Complete the 5-step onboarding — select your niche and goals
4
Connect your YouTube channel (optional but recommended)

Step 2: Add Your AI API Key

AutoTubeOS uses Groq or Google Gemini to generate scripts and topics. Both have generous free tiers.

ℹ️ Note
Get your free Groq API key at console.groq.com. It starts with "gsk_" and gives you 14,400 tokens/minute free.
Settings → Integrations → Groq API Key → Paste key → Save

Step 3: Find Your First Video Idea

Go to Topic Finder, type your niche (e.g. "personal finance"), and click Find Topics. AI will return 8+ ranked ideas within seconds.

💡 Pro Tip
Sort by "Low Competition + High RPM" for the best first video ideas. Anything with a score above 8.0 is worth pursuing.

Step 4: Generate a Script

Click "Write Script" on any topic idea, choose your format (Listicle, Documentary, Finance, etc.), and hit Generate. Your full script streams in under 30 seconds.

That's it — you're ready to create your first video!
